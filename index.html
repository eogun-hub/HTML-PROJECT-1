About Me

Hello, my name is Ogunsola Emmanuel. I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Caleb University with a deep passion for software engineering, secure coding principles, and cybersecurity. Inspired to bridge the gap between robust software development and automated threat defense, I am actively building my expertise across Java, Python, and web technologies with the ultimate goal of becoming an Application Security (AppSec) Engineer.

Beyond my technical coursework, I channel my creativity into digital storytelling and worldbuilding, such as authoring my supernatural fiction novel The Split Heir, which gives me a strong eye for detail, structural design, and methodical problem-solving in everything I build.
